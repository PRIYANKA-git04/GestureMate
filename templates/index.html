Chess Basics

Rules of the Chess Game

Learn the basic rules you need before starting your GestureChess match.

1

Objective

Checkmate the opponent’s king so that it has no legal way to escape.

2

White Moves First

The player controlling the white pieces always begins the game.

3

Legal Movement

Every piece must move according to its specific movement pattern.

4

Capturing Pieces

Capture an opponent’s piece by moving onto the square occupied by it.

5

Checkmate

The game ends when a checked king has no valid move available.

6

Draw or Stalemate

The game may end in a draw when neither player can achieve checkmate.
